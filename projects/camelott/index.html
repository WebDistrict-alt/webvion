Chapter I The Legend

A hall where every guest is seated as royalty

Long ago, a king gathered his knights around a table with no head — so that no one would sit above another. CAMELOTT was born from that idea. Behind heavy oak doors on Pendragon Street, beneath vaulted stone and the glow of four hundred candles, we serve a feast where every guest is honoured equally.

Our kitchen reads the old chronicles the way a chef reads a pantry: venison and wild herbs from the forests of the realm, salt-baked fish from the lake, honey, saffron and smoke. Each recipe is reimagined with modern precision — a medieval soul in a contemporary body.
